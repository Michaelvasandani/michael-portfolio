Type: prototype

## Question

How should resume-derived sections, additive Project Profiles, and the Recent Work Digest be organized and presented within the GitHub Pages Minimal theme so hiring managers can scan outcomes quickly while technical interviewers can inspect credible detail? Produce a rough artifact for live human review.

## Answer

Use the revised **Outcome Funnel** information architecture from [the reviewed prototype](../prototypes/portfolio-information-architecture/index.html?variant=A).

The page begins with a concise positioning statement and contact links, followed by this fixed content order:

1. **About me** — protected, human-authored context, including education and a short human-authored space for interests beyond work.
2. **Experience** — resume-derived roles presented chronologically with measurable outcomes embedded in the relevant role rather than separated into summary tiles.
3. **Projects** — additive Project Profiles that lead with purpose and outcome, expose technologies, and link to deeper technical evidence.
4. **Recent work** — the generated Recent Work Digest at the end of the page, with dated synthesized updates and links to public evidence.

Do not use detached metric cards such as “60%” or “10+ hours.” Keep those facts attached to the experience or project that substantiates them. Preserve the Minimal theme's restrained, editorial presentation: a single scan path, strong section labels, generous spacing, and technical detail available without making the first pass feel like a dashboard.
